Bio

About
Tomas.

Tomas Tobolski is an Argentine recording and front-of-house engineer based in Miami. His work spans recording, production, mixing, mastering, and live sound for DIY venues, wedding bands, and professional recital halls. He pairs the character of vintage analog recording and mixing with the speed and flexibility of a modern digital workflow.

As a guitarist, vocalist, and drummer, Tomas understands how to keep a session comfortable for performers while moving a project forward. Years of running Deep Blue Studio and producing albums for local bands have made him adaptable with both studio environments and the gear each project needs.

He is completing a B.M. in Music Professionalism with a focus on Audio Engineering at the Frost School of Music, University of Miami, with graduation planned for fall 2026. His work there includes critical listening, recording, mixing, mastering, vocal performance, music theory, audio electronics, psychoacoustics, and recital-hall infrastructure.

Tomas has served as a lead engineer at Crescent Moon Studios under Carlos Alvarez and Emilio Estefan; worked sessions for Gloria Estefan, Roxanna Amed, and Ed Calle; supported high-end wedding and corporate events with Aesir Sound; and assisted at Criteria Recording Studios. At Frost, he co-manages the L. Austin Weeks Recording Studio with Mike Lundy.
